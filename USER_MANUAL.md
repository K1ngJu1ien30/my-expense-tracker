# User Manual — Tracker Super App

Welcome to **Tracker** — your all-in-one expense tracker, asset manager, and content radar. This guide walks you through every feature so you can get the most out of the app from day one.

---

## Welcome to Your Hub

The **Hub** is your home base. Every time you open it, you'll see a **Morning Briefing** card right at the top — think of it as your financial weather report.

### The Briefing Card

- **Today's Velocity** — This is the total amount you've spent *today*. It updates in real time as you add expenses. If it says "RM 0.00" in the morning, you're starting fresh.
- **Radar Alert** — If you've set up content sources (more on that below), you'll see a small purple badge like "📡 3 New" showing how many new articles or movies dropped today. Tap it to jump straight to your feed.

### Quick Log Expense

See that big button that says **"+ Quick Log Expense"**? That's your best friend during a commute or a coffee run.

1. Tap it
2. Enter the amount (e.g., 6.50)
3. Pick a category (Food, Transport, etc.)
4. Hit save

Done. The whole thing takes about 3 seconds. Your briefing card and dashboard will update instantly to reflect the new expense.

> **Tip:** You can also tap the round **+** button in the center of the bottom navigation bar — it does the same thing from any screen.

## Tracking Your Wealth (Stats)

Tap **Stats** in the bottom navigation to open your analytics dashboard. This is where your spending habits come to life.

### Time Filters

At the top right, you'll see three buttons: **Month**, **Year**, and **All**. These control what data every chart on the page is showing. Most of the time, you'll want "Month" to see how your current month is shaping up.

### Spending Category Breakdown

The donut chart shows you exactly where your money is going — Food, Transport, Shopping, etc. — with percentages and totals. Tap any slice to see the exact amount.

### Cash Flow Chart

The bar chart below shows your daily (or monthly) spending stacked by category. This helps you spot patterns — maybe you always overspend on weekends, or your Food spending spikes mid-month.

### Spending Velocity — Your Crystal Ball

This is the most powerful chart on the page. It only appears during **Month** view.

- The **solid line** shows your actual cumulative spending so far this month
- The **dashed line** shows where you're *headed* if you keep spending at the same rate

Above the chart, you'll see a projection like:

> *"At your current pace of RM 42.50/day, you are projected to spend RM 1,275.00 this month."*

**What this means for your budget:**
- If the projected number is way above your comfort zone, it's a signal to slow down for the rest of the month
- If it's comfortably below your income, you're in great shape
- The projection recalculates every time you add a new expense, so it gets more accurate as the month progresses

## The Asset Vault

The Asset Vault helps you track the things you own — phones, laptops, headphones, shoes, cameras — and understand their true cost over time.

### How to Enable It

1. Go to **Profile** (bottom right)
2. Scroll to **Modules**
3. Turn on **Asset Vault**
4. It will now appear in your **Hub**

### Adding Your First Asset

1. Open the **Hub** → tap **Asset Vault**
2. Tap the **+** button in the top right
3. Pick an emoji icon (📱 for phone, 👟 for shoes, etc.)
4. Enter the name (e.g., "iPhone 15 Pro")
5. Choose or type a category (e.g., "Electronics")
6. Set the purchase date
7. Enter the purchase price
8. Tap **Add to Vault**

### Understanding Cost Per Day

This is where it gets interesting. The Vault automatically calculates:

> **Cost / Day** = Total Investment ÷ Days Since Purchase

So if you bought headphones for RM 350 and you've had them for 100 days, your cost per day is RM 3.50. As time goes on, that number keeps dropping — proof that quality purchases pay for themselves.

The dashboard shows the **combined Cost / Day** across all your tracked items. Use the category filter pills to see the cost per day for just your electronics, or just your audio gear, etc.

### Maintenance Logs — Track the True Cost

Real ownership costs more than the sticker price. That phone case, the screen protector, the repair bill — those all add up.

**To add a maintenance log:**

1. Tap any asset card to open its detail view
2. In the **Maintenance Log** section, tap the **+** button
3. Enter a description (e.g., "Bought silicone case")
4. Enter the cost (e.g., 45.00)
5. Set the date
6. Tap **Save Log Entry**

The log appears in a timeline below the hero card. More importantly, the **True Total** and **Cost / Day** numbers update instantly:

> **True Total** = Purchase Price + All Maintenance Costs

> **Cost / Day** = True Total ÷ Days Active

This gives you an honest picture of what each item truly costs to own.

> **Tip:** You can also edit or delete any log entry by tapping the pencil or trash icons next to it.

## The Radar Tracker

The Radar is your personal content feed — a mix of news websites and movie/streaming trackers, all in one place.

### How to Enable It

1. Go to **Profile** → **Modules** → Turn on **Radar Tracker**
2. It will now appear in your **Hub**

### Setting Up Your Sources

Tap into **Radar Tracker** from the Hub, then tap the **⚙️ gear icon** in the top right to open **Radar Sources**. This is where you build your custom feed.

Tap the **+** button to add a new source. You'll see three options:

---

#### Option 1: RSS Feed (Websites & Blogs)

This lets you pull articles from any website that has an RSS feed.

1. Select **RSS Feed**
2. Give it a name (e.g., "Photography")
3. Paste the RSS URL (e.g., `https://petapixel.com/feed/`)
4. Optionally add **keywords** to filter — comma-separated (e.g., "sony, fujifilm, canon"). Only articles matching at least one keyword will show up.
5. Tap **Add Source**

> **How to find an RSS URL:** Most blogs and news sites have one. Try adding `/feed/` or `/rss` to the end of the website's URL. You can also search "[website name] RSS feed" online.

**Some popular RSS feeds to get you started:**
- Tech: `https://www.theverge.com/rss/index.xml`
- Photography: `https://petapixel.com/feed/`
- Gaming: `https://kotaku.com/rss`

---

#### Option 2: In Cinemas (MY)

Want to know what's playing in Malaysian cinemas right now?

1. Select **In Cinemas (MY)**
2. Give it a name (e.g., "Now Showing")
3. Tap **Add Source**

That's it. The app will automatically pull the current cinema listings from TMDB's movie database, complete with poster images and release dates.

---

#### Option 3: Streaming Drops

Track new releases on your favorite streaming platforms.

1. Select **Streaming Drops**
2. Give it a name (e.g., "Netflix")
3. Pick your platform from the dropdown:
   - Netflix
   - Disney+
   - Amazon Prime Video
   - Apple TV+
   - HBO Go
   - All Major Platforms (shows everything)
4. Tap **Add Source**

Each movie card will show the platform badge (e.g., "Netflix") alongside the release date so you always know where to watch.

> **Tip:** You can add multiple streaming sources — one for Netflix, one for Disney+, etc. — and use the filter pills at the top of the feed to switch between them.

---

### Reading Your Feed

Back on the main Radar page, all your sources combine into a single, unified feed sorted by date (newest first).

- **Filter pills** at the top let you view just one source category at a time, or "All" to see everything mixed together
- **Movie cards** show a large poster image with a gradient overlay, title, and metadata
- **Article cards** show a thumbnail, headline, and snippet
- Tap any card to open the full article or movie page in your browser
- Use the **search bar** to find specific topics across all your sources

## Tips & Tricks

- **Offline Mode**: Lost signal on the train? No problem. Add expenses as normal — they'll be saved locally and automatically synced to the cloud when you're back online. You'll see a toast notification confirming the sync.
- **Dark Mode**: Go to Profile → toggle Dark Mode. Everything adapts, including charts and overlays.
- **Accent Colors**: Profile → Accent Color lets you pick from Blue, Emerald, Yellow, Purple, or Rose. This changes the look of buttons, charts, badges, and highlights across the entire app.
- **Export Your Data**: Profile → Export to CSV downloads all your transactions as a spreadsheet file — handy for tax season or personal record-keeping.
- **Pending Income**: When adding income, you can mark it as "Pending" (e.g., an invoice you've sent but haven't been paid for yet). Pending income won't count toward your total balance until you mark it as cleared.
